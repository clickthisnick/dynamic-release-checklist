Hotel checkout checklist

This file lists every conditional rule. It is not a flat todo list.
Do only the items whose conditions match the answers.
Never turn off, unplug, or disable smoke alarms or carbon monoxide alarms.
A phone alarm is a reminder on a phone, not a smoke or carbon monoxide alarm.
On the checklist page, follow only the Applicable items section
(#applicable-checklist-text). Tasks that do not apply are omitted there.

Questions:
1. Do you need to turn in the key card
   Default answer: No
2. Is the car far away?
   Default answer: No

Tasks:
1. Show if all of these answers are Yes: 1. Do you need to turn in the key card.
   Section: Pre Checkout
   Role: Mom
   Get everyones keycard

2. Always show.
   Section: Pre Checkout
   Role: Dad
   Check if any food is left in fridge

3. Always show.
   Section: Pre Checkout
   Role: Mom
   Make sure nothing is left behind in the bathroom

4. Always show.
   Section: Pre Checkout
   Role: Sister
   Check the closet to make sure nothing is left behind

5. Always show.
   Section: Pre Checkout
   Role: Brother
   Look under the beds to make sure nothing is left behind

6. Always show.
   Section: Checkout
   Role: Dad
   Carry suitcases downstairs

7. Always show.
   Section: Checkout
   Role: Brother
   Help dad carry suitecases downstairs

8. Always show.
   Section: Checkout
   Role: Brother
   Wait in lobby with suitcases

9. Show if all of these answers are Yes: 2. Is the car far away?
   Section: Checkout
   Role: Dad
   Walk to car and bring to front of hotel

10. Always show.
   Section: Checkout
   Role: Sister
   Do one final walkthrough of room to check for left items

11. Show if all of these answers are Yes: 1. Do you need to turn in the key card.
   Section: Checkout
   Role: Mom
   Give receptionist the keycards

12. Show if all of these answers are Yes: 1. Do you need to turn in the key card.
   Section: Checkout
   Role: Mom
   Check out of hotel/pay any fees
