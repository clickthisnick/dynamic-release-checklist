Moving checklist

This file lists every conditional rule. It is not a flat todo list.
Do only the items whose conditions match the answers.
Never turn off, unplug, or disable smoke alarms or carbon monoxide alarms.
A phone alarm is a reminder on a phone, not a smoke or carbon monoxide alarm.
On the checklist page, follow only the Applicable items section
(#applicable-checklist-text). Tasks that do not apply are omitted there.

Questions:
1. Do you have lamps
   Default answer: No
2. Do you have a metal bed frame
   Default answer: No
3. Do you have furniture like a dresser or tv stand with removable pieces like shelves/screws
   Default answer: No

Tasks:
1. Show if all of these answers are Yes: 1. Do you have lamps.
   Section: Moving Day
   Role: Movee
   Unscrew Lightbulbs and Pack. (Movers won"t move with lightbulbs in)

2. Show if all of these answers are Yes: 2. Do you have a metal bed frame.
   Section: Moving Day
   Role: Movee
   Fold metal bed frame, leaving screws in frame

3. Show if all of these answers are Yes: 3. Do you have furniture like a dresser or tv stand with removable pieces like shelves/screws.
   Section: Pre Move
   Role: Movee
   Put all removable screws/attachments/shelves etc from tv stands/shelves in a ziplock bag and label so they dont get lost

4. Always show.
   Section: Pre Move
   Role: Movee
   Purchase boxes, around 38 medium for a 1 bedroom

5. Always show.
   Section: Pre Move
   Role: Movee
   Purchase mattress cover

6. Always show.
   Section: Pre Move
   Role: Movee
   Confirm move with both places
